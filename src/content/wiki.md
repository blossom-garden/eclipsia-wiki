# Eclipsia

## Ancestries

In the world of Eclipsia every player is capable of choosing their [Overworld Ancestry](overworld/ancestry).

If the overworld does not offer what you wish, the [Otherworld Ancestry](otherworld/ancestry) has some more options.

Each and every ancestry comes with _Flavors_ that narrow it down your abilities and limitations in your life.
