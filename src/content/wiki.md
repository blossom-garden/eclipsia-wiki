# Eclipsia

## Ancestries

In the world of Eclipsia every player is capable of choosing their [[wiki/ancestry/overworld|Overworld Ancestry]].

If the overworld does not offer what you wish, the [[wiki/ancestry/otherworld|Otherworld Ancestry]] has some more options.

Each and every ancestry comes with _Flavors_ that narrow it down your abilities and limitations in your life.
