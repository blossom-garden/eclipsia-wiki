Apidapeans are faeries that most resemble the likes of a bee. They are the tiniest of the faeries and possibly the most resistant ones.

They posses a great resistance to poison and survive based on [[Flower Nectar]] and their own produced [[Fae Honey]].

Apidapeans might be slow on their flight but have the best stamina for it, during the day that is. They are even slower at night and being wet causes their wings to become too heavy to fly.

The lack of light of the night causes much less vision.

They are fragile and contain little points of health.

Heavy armor would not let them fly so be careful.

These worker bees are the most efficient in their jobs. Any tools crafted by them has slight faster mining speed and the faes themselves have some haste on their hands too.
